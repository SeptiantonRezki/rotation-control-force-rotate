## Full description — English

Choose the screen orientation that works for you.

Rotation Control gives you quick access to nine device-wide rotation modes and restores your saved choice when the rotation service starts again.

• Choose Auto-Rotate, Portrait, Reverse Portrait, Landscape, Sensor Landscape, Reverse Landscape, Sensor Portrait, Lock Current, or Guard Mode.
• Use the persistent notification as a shortcut back to the controls.
• Restore your last selected mode after device startup when the rotation service is enabled.
• Optionally pause the rotation override during a phone call.
• See banner and native ads within app pages. The app does not use interstitial or app-open ads.

**Permissions and compatibility**

Modify system settings is needed to change Android's device rotation settings. Phone state access is optional and is used only to detect call state for the call bypass feature; the app does not use phone numbers or call history.

Android, individual apps, and device manufacturers can apply their own rotation policies. Results can vary by device, Android version, and the app or system screen in use.
