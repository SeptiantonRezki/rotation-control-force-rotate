Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy explains how Rotation Control: Force Rotate accesses and handles information when you use the Android app. The app has no user accounts and the developer does not operate an app backend.

Information used on your device

  • Rotation settings and history. The app stores your service state, selected global rotation mode, and up to ten recent mode selections with timestamps in Android device-protected SharedPreferences. This information stays on your device and is used to apply and restore your settings.
  • Optional phone-call bypass. If you enable this setting and grant Phone state access, the app uses only IDLE, RINGING, and OFFHOOK call states to pause and resume rotation. It does not use phone numbers or call history.
  • System settings access. Android's Modify system settings access is used to update device rotation settings. The app does not use a screen overlay to control rotation.

Advertising and consent services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show adaptive banner and native ads, manage consent, and provide privacy choices. It does not use interstitial or app-open ads.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as IP address (which can be used to infer general location), advertising or app-set identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google and its advertising partners may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. The exact processing can depend on your region, consent choices, Android advertising settings, and SDK version. We do not integrate a separate analytics SDK.

UMP checks consent requirements and presents any required message before the app requests ads. Where available, you can open Privacy options from Settings. Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google UMP and consent information
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Google Play

Sharing, retention, and deletion

The developer does not receive local rotation settings, rotation history, or call-state information. Google processes information through its advertising and consent SDKs as described above and in Google's policies. We do not sell personal information.

Local settings remain on the device until you clear Rotation Control's storage or uninstall the app. The app does not create an account, so account deletion is not applicable. Google controls retention for data handled by its services; use Google's privacy and advertising controls for those requests.

Security

App preferences are stored in Android's app-private device-protected storage. Data exchanged by Google's advertising and consent services is handled under Google's security and privacy practices. The developer's app has no backend.

Children

Rotation Control is a general-purpose device utility and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising behavior may also be affected by applicable consent and platform settings.
