Privacy Policy

Rotation Control: Force Rotate for Android

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how Rotation Control: Force Rotate accesses and handles information. The app has no user accounts and the developer does not operate an app backend.

Information used on your device

  • Rotation settings and history. The app stores service state, the selected rotation mode, up to ten recent choices with timestamps, and per-app profiles in Android device-protected SharedPreferences. This information stays on your device and is used to apply or restore your settings.
  • Installed app visibility. The Per-App picker uses Android package visibility to show apps with a launcher activity. Package names are used locally for the picker and saved profiles; the app does not send your installed-app list to the developer or include it in requests the app creates for ads.
  • Accessibility service. If enabled, the service reads the package name from foreground window-state events to apply your saved profile. It does not read screen text or window content, capture screenshots, or send the package name to the developer.
  • Optional phone-call bypass. If enabled, the app uses IDLE, RINGING, and OFFHOOK states to pause and resume rotation. It does not use phone numbers or call history.
  • System settings access. Android's Modify system settings access is used to update device rotation settings. The app does not use a screen overlay to control rotation.

Android package visibility applies at the application level, including code from included SDKs. The app does not ask the advertising SDK to read or use the installed-app list. See the advertising section for Google's data practices.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show adaptive banner and native ads, manage consent, and provide privacy choices. It does not use interstitial or app-open ads.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as IP address (which can indicate general location), advertising or app-set identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google and its advertising partners may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. Processing can depend on your region, consent choices, Android settings, and SDK version. The app does not include a separate analytics SDK.

UMP checks consent requirements and presents any required message before ad requests. Where available, Privacy options appear in Settings. Read more in the Google Privacy Policy, Google UMP guidance, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

The developer does not receive local rotation history, profiles, package names, or call states. Google processes information through its advertising and consent SDKs as described above and in Google's policies. We do not sell personal information.

Local settings remain on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. The app does not create accounts. Google controls retention for information handled by its services; use Google's privacy and advertising controls for related requests.

Security

App preferences are stored in Android app-private device-protected storage. Google's advertising and consent services handle their data transfers under Google's security and privacy practices. The developer's app has no backend.

Children

Rotation Control is a general-purpose device utility and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising behavior may also be affected by applicable consent and platform settings.
